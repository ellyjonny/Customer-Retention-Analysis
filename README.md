## From sample dataset here are some of the finding from the analysis 〽️
💠Churn rate tends to decline with age, suggesting younger users are more likely to leave the service.
💠Customers who churn tend to have lower total spend, indicating a correlation between higher engagement/spending and retention.
💠Churn decreases as support calls increase. Users with more support interactions tend to stay longer, indicating effective support may improve retention.
